# Canada's Food Guide (2019) - core recommendations

## The healthy plate
- Fill about half your plate with vegetables and fruits.
- Fill about a quarter with whole grain foods.
- Fill about a quarter with protein foods, choosing plant-based protein foods more often.
- Make water your drink of choice.

## Healthy eating habits
- Be mindful of your eating habits; cook more often; enjoy your food; eat meals with others.
- Use food labels; limit foods high in sodium, sugars or saturated fat; limit highly processed foods.
- Be aware of food marketing.

## Use in this application
The meal-photo feature compares an estimated plate composition with these proportions. It gives general guidance only and is not an individual nutrition prescription. People with medical conditions should consult a registered dietitian.
