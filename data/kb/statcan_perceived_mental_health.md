# Perceived mental health

- In the 2023/2024 Canadian Community Health Survey (CCHS), 15.1% of Canadians aged 18 and over rated their mental health as fair or poor.
- Among adults aged 65 and older, 8.1% rated their mental health as fair or poor (the lowest of any age group), and 62.5% rated it very good or excellent.
- From 2015 to 2023, about 7.0% of older Canadians reported a diagnosed mood disorder.

## Data source notes
The CCHS is an annual population health survey. Public Use Microdata Files (PUMF) are available through Statistics Canada for research. Self-rated mental health is a perception measure, not a diagnosis.
