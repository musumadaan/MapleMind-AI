# Primary care access (OurCare 2025)

- The 2025 OurCare survey (16,299 respondents, April to July 2025) estimated that about 5.9 million adults in Canada lack a regular family doctor, nurse practitioner or primary care team.
- This is down from an estimated 6.5 million in the 2022 survey.
- In 2022, about 35% of adults aged 18 to 29 reported having no primary care provider.
- People without a regular provider commonly use walk-in clinics, virtual walk-in clinics or emergency departments for non-urgent problems.

## Implication
Many Canadians who screen positive for depression or anxiety, or who want help with weight-related health, have no regular clinician to follow up with. Navigation must therefore include options that do not require a family doctor (for example 811 health lines, walk-in clinics, community mental health agencies and crisis lines).
