# Crisis resources

- 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline: call or text 9-8-8, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, in English and French. It launched on November 30, 2023, is funded by the Public Health Agency of Canada and coordinated by CAMH through a network of local crisis lines. The federal government renewed funding in January 2026.
- Emergency: call 9-1-1 or go to the nearest emergency department if someone is in immediate danger.
- Kids Help Phone (young people): call 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
- Hope for Wellness Help Line (Indigenous peoples in Canada): 1-855-242-3310, or chat at hopeforwellness.ca.
- Health information lines: in most provinces, dial 8-1-1 to reach a registered nurse (for example Health811 in Ontario, HealthLink BC, Health Link Alberta, Info-Sante in Quebec).

## Rule for the assistant
If a user expresses suicidal thoughts, intent to harm themselves or others, or is in danger, stop all other tasks and provide these resources first, in a calm, non-judgemental way.
