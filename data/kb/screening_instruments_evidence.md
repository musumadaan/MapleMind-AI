# Screening questionnaires used by MapleMind

## Depression: PHQ-2 then PHQ-9
- PHQ-2 scores range 0-6; PHQ-9 scores range 0-27.
- An individual participant data meta-analysis (44 studies, 10,627 participants) found that PHQ-2 score of 2 or more followed by PHQ-9 score of 10 or more had sensitivity 0.82, specificity 0.87 and AUC 0.90, not significantly different in sensitivity from PHQ-9 alone (0.86).
- This two-stage approach lets most people answer only 2 questions, which is why MapleMind uses it to cap follow-up questions.
- PHQ-9 severity bands: 0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15-19 moderately severe, 20-27 severe.
- PHQ-9 item 9 asks about thoughts of being better off dead or self-harm; any non-zero answer requires immediate safety follow-up regardless of total score.

## Anxiety: GAD-2 then GAD-7
- Recommended cut-points are 3 or more on the GAD-2 and 10 or more on the GAD-7 (Kroenke et al. 2010). A meta-analysis found GAD-7 sensitivity 0.83 and specificity 0.84 at a cut-off of 8 for generalized anxiety disorder, with cut-offs 7-10 performing similarly.
- GAD-7 severity bands: 0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15-21 severe.

## Limits
- Screening is not diagnosis. Positive screens should be followed by a clinical assessment.
- Optimal cut-offs vary across languages and populations.
